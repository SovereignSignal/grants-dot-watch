Organization Profile
Uniswap Foundation
Foundation· Ethereum· Active· uniswapfoundation.org

Nonprofit steward supporting the Uniswap protocol, Unichain, v4 hooks, research, and governance. It runs protocol grants, ecosystem grants, a hook incubator, audit subsidies, and research programs — committing $26M in new grants and disbursing $11M in FY2025, with $106M earmarked overall.1 Additional funding has been approved via governance; the foundation plans to close operations after deploying its remaining (~$100M) grants budget.
