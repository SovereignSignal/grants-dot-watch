## Scope

This pass reviewed all 56 programs marked active even though every dated round on file had already
closed, entered review, or deployed. The review separated one-time calls and named cohorts from
recurring programs and ongoing umbrella funds.

Program status describes whether the funding vehicle remains current. A named cohort or one-time
call is concluded after its application window closes, even while selection or funded work
continues. A recurring program can remain active between rounds when its primary program page and
history establish an ongoing vehicle.
